The problem with AI roleplay

Every time you start a new session, it's like it never happened.

You've spent weeks building a story with a character. You've earned titles, made enemies, shaped the world. Then you open a new chat and they've forgotten everything — who you are, what you've been through, the history you share.

You end up re-explaining yourself every session, or writing long static system prompts that slowly drift further and further from where the story actually is.

PAC is a SillyTavern extension that solves this. It automatically captures what matters from your conversations and injects it back into every future session — so your characters actually know you the next time you talk to them.

Five layers of persistent memory

PAC organises memory into distinct stores, each injected into every prompt within a configurable token budget.

♔

Your Active Persona

Permanent facts about your player character — titles earned, faction standings, reputation, NPC relationships. This is about you.

"Champion of the Iron Circle · Allied with the Merchant's Guild · Wanted by the City Guard"
🌎

World State

World-shaping events that permanently changed the setting. Shared across all characters in the world.

"The old king was slain. The city of Vareth lies under rebel control. The northern pass is sealed by ice magic."
🤵

Character Knowledge

Permanent facts about the AI character you're talking to — their titles, affiliations, reputation. Separate from your persona.

"Now known as the Exile Knight. Former Silver Order. Carries a blade that cannot rust."
📖

Story So Far

Compressed recaps of past sessions — the "previously on…" that the AI reads before every chat. Auto-generated every 40 AI responses.

"Last session: you defeated the king's assassin in the old chapel, gained the trust of the rebel faction…"
✨

Episodic Memories

Specific moments — things that happened, places visited, conversations had. Retrieved by relevance using hybrid search.

"Rescued the merchant's daughter from the ruins beneath the market quarter"

How it works

PAC runs quietly in the background. You don't have to manage anything by hand.

Extraction — every 30 AI messages

1

Your AI classifies the conversation

PAC sends the recent exchange to your AI model and asks it to identify notable facts — who you've become, what the character now knows, what changed in the world, what moments happened.

2

You approve what gets saved

A dialog shows the proposed entries across all memory buckets. Tick or untick anything before saving. You're always in control of what makes it into permanent memory.

3

Stored locally and indexed

Approved entries are written to plain JSON/JSONL files on your machine and added to the search index. Nothing leaves your device.

Injection — at every generation

1

Relevant memories are retrieved

PAC runs a hybrid search — BM25 keyword matching plus optional semantic vector search — and merges results. The memories most relevant to the current conversation surface first.

2

All layers are assembled

Persona facts, world state, character knowledge, session summaries, and top memories are packed together within your configured token budget.

3

Injected into the prompt

The assembled context is quietly inserted before the AI generates its next response. It sees your shared history as natural background knowledge.

Everything in the box

PAC ships as a complete system — extraction, storage, retrieval, and injection all built in.

✅

Approval dialogs

Review every proposed memory before it's saved. Tick or untick individual items across all four buckets.

🔍

Hybrid memory search

BM25 keyword matching plus optional semantic vector embeddings, merged with Reciprocal Rank Fusion.

🌐

Persistent World mode

Share world and character memory across multiple personas. Your personal profile always stays private.

🏢

Token budget control

Set a total token budget for PAC with per-layer minimums. It never crowds out your actual conversation.

📄

Auto session summaries

Every 40 AI responses, a compressed recap is generated and stored. The two most recent summaries inject into every prompt.

⚙

Memory consolidation

Merge groups of related memories into single compact entries to prevent log bloat over long campaigns.

💾

Import & export

Back up and restore all memory data as a zip, per persona. Human-readable JSON/JSONL on disk at all times.

👁

Preview tab

See exactly what text will be injected into the next prompt, broken down by layer, before you send anything.

🏷

World tag scoping

PAC is completely dormant unless a character has a matching world tag. No accidental memory for random chats.

🔒

Fully local

All memory is stored on your machine. No cloud, no accounts, no telemetry. Works with any AI provider.

Installation

PAC has two parts: the client extension and a server plugin. Both must be installed.

Important: The server plugin cannot be installed automatically — it always requires a manual step regardless of which installation method you use. SillyTavern must be restarted after both parts are in place.
1

Install the client extension via SillyTavern

In SillyTavern, open Extensions → Install Extension, paste the URL below, and click Install.

https://github.com/GombaTheGreat/PAC-Persistent-Adventure-Chronicle
2

Manually install the server plugin

Download or clone this repo and copy the server-plugin/ folder into your SillyTavern plugins directory:

SillyTavern/ └── plugins/ └── pac/ └── index.mjs
3

Enable server plugins in config.yaml

Open config.yaml in your SillyTavern root and add or confirm:

enableServerPlugins: true
4

Restart SillyTavern fully

Close and reopen SillyTavern — don't just refresh the browser tab. The server plugin loads on startup.

5

Enable PAC in Extensions

Go to Extensions (puzzle piece icon) and make sure Persistent Adventure Chronicle is toggled on. A PAC panel will appear in your sidebar.

1

Download the release zip

Grab the latest zip from the Releases page.

2

Extract into your SillyTavern root folder

The SillyTavern root is the folder containing Start.bat or start.sh. When prompted to merge folders, say Yes.

SillyTavern/ ├── data/default-user/extensions/pac/ ← client extension └── plugins/pac/ ← server plugin
3

Enable server plugins & restart

Add enableServerPlugins: true to config.yaml, then restart SillyTavern fully.

4

Enable PAC in Extensions

Toggle on Persistent Adventure Chronicle in the Extensions panel.

Non-default username? If you've changed your SillyTavern username from the default, also copy extensions/pac/ from data/default-user/ into data/YOUR-USERNAME/extensions/.

First-time setup — world tags

PAC only activates for characters that belong to a recognised world. This prevents accidental memory for every character you open. Setup takes thirty seconds:

1

Add a world name in PAC → General → World Tags

e.g. Eldoria, Voruun, CyberCity — whatever suits your campaign.

2

Add the same word as a tag on your character card

Open the character in SillyTavern's editor, add the tag, save.

3

Open a chat — PAC activates automatically

The status bar will show 🌍 Eldoria | CharacterName | 0 memories. You're tracking.

Why I made this

SillyTavern is brilliant for roleplay and collaborative storytelling with AI. But the amnesia between sessions breaks immersion in a way that's hard to paper over. You can write long system prompts that describe who you are — but they're static, they don't grow with your story, and maintaining them by hand becomes a real chore the longer a campaign runs.

PAC is what I wanted to exist: something that quietly watches your story unfold, pulls out what matters, and makes sure your characters actually know you the next time you talk to them. The AI you've been building a campaign with should know you're the Champion of the Iron Circle before you have to tell it again.

I made this for people who want their AI adventures to feel like they're going somewhere — where the characters you care about remember the journey.

— GombaTheGreat
